title=AKA - I Know What I Did [B42]
description=[h2]A diary that remembers only what you actually earned[/h2]
description=
description=Your traits and your occupation gave you levels at birth. This mod does not care about those.
description=It writes down [b]only what your own hands learned by playing[/b] - and lets the next you read it back.
description=
description=[h3]How it works[/h3]
description=[olist]
description=[*] Craft a [b]Life Diary[/b]: 1 Diary + 3 Phonebooks + 2 units of Adhesive Tape. It never spawns as loot.
description=[*] Open the new diary. Fill in the form: your character name is filled in for you, you pick the nickname, then sign it.
description=[*] [b]Start recording skills[/b] - writes today's earned XP into the book. Press it again any time you want it up to date.
description=[*] You die. Your next character finds the diary and picks [b]Remember my past[/b].
description=[*] The XP comes back at the percentage the admin set. The diary is not destroyed.
description=[/olist]
description=
description=[h3]Rules you can change (Sandbox Options)[/h3]
description=[list]
description=[*] Restore percent (default 50)
description=[*] Seconds spent writing / reading (default 60)
description=[*] One restore per character (default on)
description=[*] Include passive skills - Strength and Fitness (default off)
description=[*] Remind me to write, after N days (default 3, 0 = never)
description=[*] Write itself up to date every N days (default off)
description=[*] Freeze skills of characters that already existed when the mod was installed (default on)
description=[/list]
description=
description=[h3]It cannot lose your work[/h3]
description=The record belongs to your [b]account[/b], not to the item, so dropping the book, storing it,
description=or dying with it in your bag changes nothing. Writing in it again never erases the life before:
description=every skill keeps whichever value is higher, so a fresh character who writes on day 2 cannot
description=wipe out the carpenter who died on day 300.
description=
description=[h3]Admin tools[/h3]
description=Right click the ground as a full admin (admin only - not moderator or GM) and pick [b]AKA Diary (admin)[/b] - then a player, then a job:
description=see what is in their diary, write everything they currently know into it, hand the skills back
description=on the spot at 100% or at the sandbox percent, count skills they had before the mod as earned,
description=let them read again, or erase the record. The server re-checks access level on every command.
description=
description=[h3]Notes[/h3]
description=[list]
description=[*] The diary [b]never spawns as loot[/b]. Crafting is the only way to get one.
description=[*] You cannot read a diary that your current character wrote - no free XP loops.
description=[*] Build 42 only. Multiplayer ready: the server decides who may restore and remembers it across relogs.
description=[*] Part of the [b]AKA[/b] mod line.
description=[/list]
