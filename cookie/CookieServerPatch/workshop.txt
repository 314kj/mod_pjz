HDs Food Recipes with one of its spawn options off: bags rolled inside containers no longer throw an error each time (its loot filter runs only on real containers, in the small CookieEarlyHooks mod). Working vehicles can be dismantled for scrap like a car wreck (welding mask + propane torch, Mechanics 6 and Electrical 3, 20% more salvage than a wreck); vehicles claimed with Another Vehicle Claim System, with someone inside or with items left inside cannot be. Copies that were already found are removed too (carried, worn or held by a player, and on the floor, in furniture or on bodies next to a player; a removed bag's contents stay). Only the ones handed out by Cookie Express are kept. Nyx Strider: the cracked-glass check looks through the loaded vehicles instead of reading 3,721 squares around each player every half second (glass looks the same). The Removed Mod Tiles part keeps the tiles and build-entity names of mods taken off the server (Building Clipboard, Blackout Predators Extended, Ammo Maker, Kentucky Cannabis, Monorails) so the world still loads and built objects keep their picture.
