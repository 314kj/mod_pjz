description=[h2]This build - UI revision[/h2]
description=[b]Personal tax and house tax now share one screen.[/b] No more right-clicking in the right spot to reach the right list - open the Billing Center once and both columns are in front of you, under a single outstanding total.
description=[list]
description=[*][b]Bill rows you can scan.[/b] Icon, number, countdown, amount, status. The row you select opens up to show where the numbers came from and what you can do about it; the rest stay compact.
description=[*][b]A countdown you can read out of the corner of your eye.[/b] Blocks that go dark as the due date approaches, and again on the way to the utility cut-off.
description=[*][b]Receipts that show their arithmetic.[/b] Tiles times rate, members over quota, late fee per day - instead of one total you have to take on faith.
description=[*][b]It tells you before you click.[/b] Bills held back by the pay-oldest-first rule are marked QUEUED, tenants see who owes the house bill instead of a greyed-out button, and a payment method the server has switched off is simply not offered.
description=[*][b]Pixel art icon set[/b] and Thai status labels, so the screen reads at a glance instead of as a wall of text.
description=[/list]
