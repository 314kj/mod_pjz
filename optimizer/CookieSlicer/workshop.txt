description=[h2]In game[/h2]
description=The [b]Cookie Slicer[/b] button on the main menu, or [b]F8[/b] in game (change it in Options > Mods), opens one big black / mint window, in Thai or English, with three pages:
description=[list]
description=[*] [b]Performance[/b]: your PC now (FPS, game RAM, PC RAM, CPU, game main thread). Point at a number to see what it means and how yours looks.
description=[*] Choose a level: [b]Undo all / Balanced / Smooth / Max[/b], or switch each setting on and off. They are the game's own options that cost CPU and RAM for little you can see (unseen zombies update less, plain zombie clothes, no blood on weapons, windows drawn 30 times a second, lighting 15 times a second, at most 10 falling bodies; weak PC after a restart: compressed textures, textures up to 1024, no corpse shadows). Your character looks the same.
description=[*] Before you even start the game it shows what the chosen settings should save (about), and after a change in game it measures your FPS before and after.
description=[*] [b]Shader[/b] (needs the Cookie Slicer Helper v1.1.0): a real screen shader drawn right after the game's own one, before any window. 8 styles: Natural, Golden Hour, True Film, Night Glow, Vivid, Cinema, Retro, Intense Dark. Each has sliders: strength, brightness, contrast, colour, warm / cool, glow, dark edges, sharpness, film grain.
description=[*] Shows whether your PC can run it: helper, graphics card, OpenGL, and the measured time on your graphics card per frame. Quality Auto / Low / Medium / High, and [b]Fit my PC[/b] keeps your style and picks the quality your card can carry.
description=[*] Compare before / after on the live game: the window moves to the left, a line on the game shows the game's picture on one side and the style on the other.
description=[*] Uses about 11 MB of graphics memory at 1080p, no PC RAM. Night vision goggles stay as the game draws them.
description=[*] Without the helper: basic looks Natural, Cinema, Retro, Intense Dark plus brightness, on your screen only, no FPS cost.
description=[*] [b]Version Slicer[/b]: mod and helper versions, game RAM, how to install, update or remove the helper.
description=[*] Smoother map loading: the work mods do for every map square that loads runs a little at a time instead of all in one frame.
description=[/list]
